# Nana's Nest editorial standard

Experience-led, evidence-supported, parent-validated.

## Publication gates
- Obtain attributable caregiver input; distinguish actual experience from illustrative scenarios.
- Document parent experiences with source links or consented interviews. Never fabricate testimonials, quotes, credentials or outcomes.
- Treat community anecdotes as qualitative evidence, not proof of effectiveness.
- Verify infant safety and clinical statements against current authoritative guidance; obtain appropriate professional review before publication.
- Provide practical steps, common mistakes, escalation criteria, and a relevant service link.
- Record actual author, reviewer, source dates and publication dates. Do not invent these.
- Check distinct search intent, canonical URL, Article metadata, internal links, mobile rendering and sitemap inclusion.
- Owner authorized posting preliminary editions for later caregiver editing on 2026-11-25. Label review status transparently; do not claim professional review or firsthand experience.

## Interview prompts
Ask caregivers what families struggle with, what they tried, what changed, what did not work, and what safety boundaries apply. Obtain permission before using identifiable details. Ask parents for the same sequence in their own words, including unsuccessful approaches. Record context and avoid claiming causality from individual accounts.
